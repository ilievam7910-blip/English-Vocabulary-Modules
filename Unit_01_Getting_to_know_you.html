The Teacher Who Changed Our Class

When Ms Carter joined our school, she looked rather serious. Some students thought she would be strict, but we soon discovered how considerate and generous she was with her time. She listened carefully and remained respectful even when we made mistakes. Although she was an intelligent and brilliant teacher, she was also humble: she never claimed to know everything.

Our class included very different personalities. Daniel was studious and worked hard, while Mia was imaginative and always suggested unusual stories. Leo was creative but sometimes stubborn; he refused to change his ideas. At first, I was rather gullible and believed every rumour about exams. My friend was more carefree, although that sometimes meant she forgot deadlines.

One day, our group project went wrong. A student made a selfish decision and kept important materials for himself. Ms Carter encouraged us to be brave enough to discuss the problem openly. She asked us to remain loyal to our team, not to our mistakes. When the computer stopped working, she was resourceful and turned the lesson into a lively discussion. By the end, even the quietest students felt self-confident enough to share their opinions. We learned that good character is shown through actions, not labels.
